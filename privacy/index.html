Second Mind Upload

Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

  • Personal tool
  • No data collected
  • My own Google Drive
  • No ads or tracking

Second Mind Upload is a personal tool that only I use. It isn't offered to anyone else, and it doesn't collect anyone's personal information.

What it does

It runs the free program rclone on my own PC to copy my own notes to my own Google Drive. It has no server of its own, and nothing is sent anywhere except my Google Drive.

Google data

It uses Google's drive.file permission, which only lets it see and change the files it created. It can't see anything else in my Drive. It doesn't share Google data with anyone or use it for ads. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in Google Account permissions.
